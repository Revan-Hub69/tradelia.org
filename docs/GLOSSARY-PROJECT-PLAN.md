# Piano Progetto Glossario Tradelia

## Obiettivo

Creare un glossario finanziario completo, rigoroso accademicamente ma accessibile, organizzato per percorso educativo progressivo.

**Target: 300-500 termini** (fase iniziale, focus qualità)

## Distribuzione Termini per Categoria

### 🔴 Priorità Alta (Fase 1-2)

#### 1. FOUNDATIONS (Fondamenta)

- **Target: 40-50 termini**
- **Livello: Foundational**
- **Utenti: Tutti (beginner → professional)**
- **Esempi**: Portfolio, Rischio, Rendimento, Volatilità, Liquidità, Asset, Equity, P&L, ROI, Drawdown, Leverage, Margin, Spread, Bid-Ask, Market Order, Limit Order, Stop Order, Long/Short, Bull/Bear Market, Correzione, Rally, Support/Resistance, Trend, Range, Breakout, Consolidamento, Volume, Open Interest, Market Cap, P/E Ratio, Dividend Yield, Yield, Coupon, Maturity, Duration, Convexity, Beta, Alpha, Correlation, Diversification, Asset Allocation, Rebalancing

#### 2. MARKET_ANALYSIS (Analisi di Mercato)

- **Target: 60-80 termini**
- **Livello: Intermediate**
- **Utenti: Intermediate-trader, Professional**
- **Subcategorie**:
  - **Technical (30-40)**: ADX, ATR, RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, Moving Averages, Ichimoku, Fibonacci, Volume Profile, OBV, Williams %R, Parabolic SAR, CCI, Aroon, Stochastic, Momentum, Divergence, Pattern Recognition, Candlestick Patterns, Chart Patterns, Support/Resistance, Trend Lines, Channels, Triangles, Head & Shoulders, Double Top/Bottom, Flags, Pennants, Wedges, Gaps, Reversal Patterns, Continuation Patterns
  - **Fundamental (15-20)**: P/E, P/B, P/S, EV/EBITDA, PEG Ratio, ROE, ROA, EPS, Revenue Growth, Profit Margin, Operating Margin, Free Cash Flow, Debt-to-Equity, Current Ratio, Quick Ratio, Book Value, Intrinsic Value, DCF, Comparable Companies, Sector Analysis, Industry Analysis
  - **Sentiment (10-15)**: VIX, Put/Call Ratio, AAII Sentiment, Fear & Greed Index, Insider Trading, Short Interest, Options Flow, Commitment of Traders
  - **Quantitative (10-15)**: Regression Analysis, Factor Models, Risk Models, VaR, CVaR, Sharpe Ratio, Sortino Ratio, Information Ratio, Treynor Ratio, Calmar Ratio, Maximum Drawdown, Correlation Matrix, Covariance

#### 3. RISK_MANAGEMENT (Gestione del Rischio)

- **Target: 40-50 termini**
- **Livello: Intermediate**
- **Utenti: Intermediate-trader, Professional**
- **Subcategorie**:
  - **Position Sizing (10-15)**: Kelly Criterion, Fixed Fractional, Fixed Ratio, Volatility-Based Sizing, Risk Parity, Equal Weight
  - **Stop Loss (10-15)**: Stop Loss, Trailing Stop, Time Stop, Volatility Stop, Percentage Stop, ATR-Based Stop, Support/Resistance Stop
  - **Diversification (10-15)**: Diversification, Correlation, Uncorrelated Assets, Asset Allocation, Geographic Diversification, Sector Diversification, Time Diversification
  - **Hedging (10-15)**: Hedge, Delta Hedge, Beta Hedge, Currency Hedge, Portfolio Hedge, Options Hedge, Futures Hedge, Cross Hedge

### 🟠 Priorità Media (Fase 2-3)

#### 4. PORTFOLIO_CONSTRUCTION (Costruzione Portafoglio)

- **Target: 40-50 termini**
- **Livello: Intermediate**
- **Utenti: Intermediate-trader, Professional**
- **Subcategorie**:
  - **Asset Allocation (15-20)**: Strategic Allocation, Tactical Allocation, Dynamic Allocation, Core-Satellite, 60/40 Portfolio, Risk Parity, Minimum Variance, Maximum Diversification
  - **Rebalancing (10-15)**: Rebalancing, Calendar Rebalancing, Threshold Rebalancing, Volatility-Based Rebalancing, Drift-Based Rebalancing
  - **Optimization (10-15)**: Mean-Variance Optimization, Black-Litterman, Risk Budgeting, Factor-Based Optimization, Constraints Optimization
  - **Performance (10-15)**: Sharpe Ratio, Sortino Ratio, Information Ratio, Alpha, Beta, Tracking Error, Active Share, Turnover, Tax Efficiency

#### 5. TRADING_EXECUTION (Trading ed Esecuzione)

- **Target: 30-40 termini**
- **Livello: Advanced**
- **Utenti: Intermediate-trader, Professional**
- **Subcategorie**:
  - **Entry Strategies (10-15)**: Breakout Entry, Pullback Entry, Momentum Entry, Mean Reversion Entry, Pattern-Based Entry

#### 6. BEHAVIORAL_FINANCE (Finanza Comportamentale)

- **Target: 30-40 termini**
- **Livello: Intermediate**
- **Utenti: Beginner, Intermediate-trader, Professional**

## Organizzazione Lavoro per Branch

### Branch Strategy

Ogni categoria avrà un branch dedicato per organizzare il lavoro:

```
glossary/foundations
glossary/market-analysis
glossary/risk-management
glossary/portfolio-construction
glossary/trading-execution
glossary/behavioral-finance
glossary/macroeconomics
glossary/valuation-pricing
glossary/derivatives-options
glossary/market-regimes
glossary/market-microstructure
glossary/compliance-regulation
```

### Workflow

1. **Creare branch** per categoria
2. **Aggiungere termini** nel branch (10-20 per volta)
3. **Review e merge** quando categoria completa
4. **Test** nell'applicazione
5. **Merge in main** quando validato
